# Daily Kharcha 💰

Daily Kharcha is a modern, simple, and user-friendly personal finance management app designed to help users manage their daily money easily.

## Features

* 💰 Income & Expense Management
* 📊 Daily, Weekly, Monthly & Yearly Reports
* 🎯 Monthly Budget Management
* 💵 Savings Goals
* 💳 Udhaar / Lend & Borrow Tracking
* 🔔 Bill & Payment Reminders
* 🛒 Shopping List
* 🚗 Vehicle & Fuel Expense Tracking
* 🔄 Recurring Transactions
* 🔎 Search & Advanced Filters
* 📈 Financial Charts & Analytics
* 🔐 PIN & Biometric Security
* ☁️ Backup & Restore
* 📄 Data Export & Import
* 🌐 English & Urdu Language Support
* 🌙 Light & Dark Mode
* 📱 Offline-First Functionality
* 📢 AdMob & Rewarded Ads Support

All financial calculations are based on real user transactions. No fake or demo financial data is included.

## Design

Daily Kharcha uses a modern green/teal finance-focused design with:

* Clean dashboard
* Rounded cards
* Modern charts
* Quick actions
* Simple navigation
* Responsive layouts
* Professional light and dark themes

## Goal

The goal of Daily Kharcha is to make personal money management simple, fast, private, and accessible for everyday users.
